Liquid Glitter Headphones

Headphones

How do I turn my headphones on or off?

Press and hold the top button on the ear cup to power on or off. A single press of the same button changes the input mode.

How do I pair them with my phone?
  1. Turn the headphones on.
  2. On your phone, open Bluetooth settings and select the headphones from the list of available devices.
  3. If they don't show up, turn the headphones off and back on, and keep them close to your phone while pairing.
They won't connect or keep disconnecting.
  • In your phone's Bluetooth settings, choose Forget this device, then pair again.
  • Turn Bluetooth off and on, or restart your phone.
  • Disconnect the headphones from any other phone, tablet or laptop they paired with before.
  • Charge the headphones. A low battery can cause dropouts.
  • Stay close to your device and avoid walls or other obstacles between you.
What do the buttons do?
Top button
Press once to change input mode. Press and hold to power on or off.
Second button
Press once to play or pause music, or answer or end a call. Press and hold to decline a call.
Third button
Press once to skip to the previous track. Press and hold to lower the volume.
Bottom button
Press once to skip to the next track. Press and hold to raise the volume.
There's no sound, or the volume is low.
  • Make sure the headphones are selected as the audio output on your phone.
  • Turn the volume up on your phone, then press and hold the bottom button to raise it on the headphones.
  • Press the top button once in case the input mode was switched.
People can't hear me on calls.
  • Check that the headphones are selected for calls in your phone's audio settings, and that you aren't muted in the call or app.
  • Disconnect and reconnect the headphones, then try the call again.
How do I charge them, and how long does the battery last?

Charge with the included USB-C cable. A full charge gives up to 6–11 hours of play, depending on volume and use. If the headphones won't turn on, charge them for a while before trying again.

Is the glitter sealed inside?

Yes. The glitter floats inside sealed, clear ear cup shells. It settles when the headphones sit still, so give them a gentle tilt to get it moving again.

LED Charger Stand

How do I set it up?

It arrives assembled. Plug the included USB power cord into the base, then into a USB wall adapter or other USB power source. Turn the light on with the ON button on the remote.

My phone isn't charging.
  • Place your phone flat and centered on the charging pad, screen up, so the camera bump doesn't tilt it. Small shifts can stop charging.
  • Remove metal cases, magnetic accessories, card wallets or phone grips, and very thick cases.
  • Check that your phone supports wireless charging (see compatible devices below).
  • Power the base from a USB wall adapter. Some computer USB ports don't supply enough power.
Charging is slow.

Wireless charging is slower than charging with a cable. For the best speed, center the phone on the pad, take off thick cases, and power the base from a USB wall adapter.

Which devices can it charge?

The charging pad works with iPhone 11 through 17, AirPods (2nd and 3rd gen) in a wireless charging case, and Android devices that support wireless charging. The stand holds your headphones but doesn't charge them.

How do I change the colors, modes or brightness?

Use the included infrared remote:

  • ON and OFF turn the light on and off.
  • The color buttons pick one of 16 colors.
  • Chase, Strobe, Fade and Smooth switch between the 4 light modes.
  • The brightness buttons at the top dim or brighten the light.
The remote isn't working.
  • Point the remote straight at the base with nothing in between. It uses infrared, so it needs a clear line of sight.
  • If there's a plastic tab in the battery compartment, pull it out.
  • Replace the remote's battery if it's still not responding.
Will my headphones fit on the stand?

The stand is designed as a universal holder for most over-ear headphones.

Battery safety: the remote contains a coin battery. Swallowing one can cause serious injury, so keep new and used batteries out of reach of children and get medical help right away if one is swallowed.
